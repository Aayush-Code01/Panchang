Shubhly

Your privacy

Shubhly works out your day on your phone. Your birth details stay with you.

  • Your name and birth details are saved only on your phone. We don't have a server that receives them.
  • No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. We never sell or share your information.
  • Your location is used only if you allow it, to give timings for where you are.
  • Delete the app, or clear its data, and everything it saved is gone.

Who we are

Shubhly is published by Healway Global Private Limited, a company registered in India (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what the Shubhly app and website do with information about you.

What stays on your phone

When you set up Shubhly you enter your first name, date of birth, time of birth (optional) and place of birth. The app uses these to work out your birth star, moon sign and rising sign, and to personalise each day. They are stored in the app's own storage on your device, together with your settings, notes, intentions, practice and chant counts, and any profiles you add for family or a partner. None of this is sent to us.

Your location

If you tap “Use my location”, the app asks your phone's permission and uses your position to calculate sunrise, sunset and good times for where you are. You can say no and pick a city instead, and you can turn this off at any time in Settings or in your phone's settings. Your position is not sent to us.

What we don't do

We don't run accounts, collect analytics, show ads, use advertising identifiers, track you across apps or websites, or sell, rent or share your information with anyone.

Children

Shubhly is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Parents can set up the app for a child; the details stay on the parent's phone.

Your choices

You can change your birth details at any time in Settings. To remove everything, delete the app or clear its data in your phone's settings. Because we don't hold your information on our side, there is nothing for us to export or delete, but you are always welcome to ask us about it.

About the readings

Shubhly offers traditional Hindu timing (panchang) and gentle guidance for reflection and planning. It is not medical, legal or financial advice.
